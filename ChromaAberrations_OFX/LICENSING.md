1. **GNU General Public License v3** (`LICENSE` file): free use, modification and redistribution,

2. **Commercial license**: for studios and vendors who want to integrate or distribute the plug-in without the

To keep the dual license possible:

## Data

| Data | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| Glass catalogue (`src/lens/glasscatalog.inc`, 1611 Schott, Ohara, Hoya, CDGM, Hikari and Sumita glasses) | refractiveindex.info database, generated by `tools/gen_glass_catalog.py` | public domain (CC0 1.0) |
| Prescriptions of the built-in lenses (`lenses/`) | US patent 8,879,901 and M. Laikin, *Lens Design* (4th ed., CRC Press 2007), as published in the Optiland examples | published technical data; Optiland transcription under the MIT license (Kramer Harrison) |
| Test reference data (`tests/reference`) | computed with Optiland | MIT |
| Spectral sensitivities of 52 cameras and 190 training reflectances (`src/core/cameras.inc`) | rawtoaces-data, Academy Software Foundation, generated by `tools/gen_cameras.py` | Apache-2.0 (`third_party/rawtoaces-data/LICENSE`): compatible with the GPL v3 and with commercial use; keep the license and the attribution |

For the commercial license: published optical prescriptions (expired patents or books) are technical data;
still, before a commercial distribution, check that no publisher claims rights on their compilation.

The analytic fits of the CIE 1931 colour matching functions (Wyman, Sloan and Shirley, 2013) and the Sellmeier
formula of N-BK7 glass (published Schott data) are published formulas, rewritten in this code.
